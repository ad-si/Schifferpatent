## Equipment

The following items should be carried on board at all times.

---

### Papers

Boat licence, registration papers.

---

### Safety Equipment

-   Anchor with sufficient line
-   Boat hook
-   2 mooring lines
-   2 paddles
-   Manual bailing device (hand pump or bailer)
-   Mouth-blown signal horn
-   Red flag (60 cm x 60 cm)
-   White all-round emergency light
-   One life jacket per crew member
-   Lifebuoy with throwing line
-   Compass
-   First-aid kit
-   2 kg fire extinguisher for inboards over 4.4 kW, outboards over
    7.4 kW, and boats with cooking or heating facilities
-   Tool kit
