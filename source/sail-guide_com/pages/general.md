## General

---

This section covers the general knowledge that every skipper needs,
regardless of whether under sail or under engine.

It includes the equipment that should be carried on board, the
navigation lights of the various vessel types, the right-of-way
rules, knots, anchoring, navigation, weather, sound signals, and
emergency signals.
